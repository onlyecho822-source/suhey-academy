Character design · Series art

The characters
come alive.

Suhey — Explorer gear, DR flag scarf, pointing the way. Always leading the adventure, always teaching through experience.
DALE — Round glasses, DR flag cape, holding a glowing golden orb — his Language Sight power. He sees the meaning inside every word.
Multiple expressions — curious, confident, surprised, thinking. A full character with a full emotional range.
Adventure ready — The same characters appear in every episode, every book, every pop-up — one consistent universe.
The catchphrase — every episode ends with DALE holding up the golden orb and shouting: ¡DALE pa' la aventura! Kids shout it back.
