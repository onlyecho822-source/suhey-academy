Suhey
The Teacher · Puerto Plata, DR

Born and raised in Puerto Plata. Part of the Tejada family — roots from Santiago to Santo Domingo. She teaches Spanish the way the brain actually learns it: structure first, words second, immersion always. She never says you are learning something new. She reveals what you already carry.

"You already know. You just have to listen and remember."
D.A.L.E.
The Guide · Sent by the Jutia

No foxes are native to Hispaniola — which means DALE was sent. The Jutia, the island's sacred native animal, prayed for someone to carry Dominican words out into the world. A fox arrived wearing the DR flag and holding maracas. He knows every word. He shows the meaning inside each one. He shouts the same word before every adventure.

"¡DALE pa' la aventura!"
