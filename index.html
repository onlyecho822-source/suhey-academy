How Spanish Actually Works

Como Funciona el Espanol

The words carry
everything.

Las palabras lo
dicen todo.

In English you need extra words. In Spanish the meaning is already inside the word — including who is speaking, when, and how many people.

En ingles necesitas palabras extra. En espanol el significado ya esta dentro de la palabra.

RULE 1 — Adjectives flip to after the noun

REGLA 1 — Los adjetivos van despues

English: The RED house. — adjective before noun.
Spanish: La casa ROJA. — adjective after noun. The order flips.

Ingles: The RED house.
Espanol: La casa ROJA. — el adjetivo va despues.

Tap any row to highlight the flip

RULE 2 — The subject is inside the verb

REGLA 2 — El sujeto vive dentro del verbo

English needs two words: I speak. Spanish just needs one: Hablo. The ending -o means it is I. The ending carries who is speaking — you never need to add the subject word.

El ingles necesita dos palabras. El espanol solo una. La terminacion lleva quien habla.

RULE 3 — One word carries a whole feeling

REGLA 3 — Una palabra carga un sentimiento entero

Some Spanish words pack meaning that takes a full English sentence to explain. Tap each word to unpack it.

Algunas palabras cargan lo que en ingles requiere toda una oracion. Toca cada una.

"Una vez que ves como esta construido el espanol, todo encaja."

Phonemic Calibration
Tapped R
"pero" (but) — one soft tap, not a roll. Tongue barely touches.
Pure vowels
"está" = eh-STAH, never eh-STAY. Spanish vowels never glide.
B and V
"boca" and "vaca" — identical sound. Soft bilabial, never teeth on lip.
Silent H
"hola" = OH-lah. H is always silent. Always.
Syllable rhythm
com-pu-ta-do-ra — equal beats. This alone removes 60% of accent.
