How the Pop-Up Books Work
1
You open the page — a 3D scene rises from the paper. A colmado appears. A palm tree unfolds. A bachata couple spins up from the spread.
2
Each element is labeled in English and Spanish. Your child points and says the word. Suhey's method — immersion, not memorization.
3
Every spread builds vocabulary — market, beach, school, family, home, DR foods. Old-school pop-up technique, new-school bilingual learning.
4
QR codes on each page link to a Suhey voice note — hearing the real Dominican pronunciation of every word in the scene.

Season 1 · Episode 1 · Introducing
¡DALE! — The Beginning
22 min · Ages 4–10
DALE arrives at Suhey's door in Puerto Plata. He says his full name. No one understands. He says: ¡DALE! Everyone understands. They explore Puerto Plata — the Amber Coast, the colonial zone, Grandma T's house on the hill. La Jutia watches from the Ceiba tree.
