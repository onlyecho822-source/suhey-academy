# Buzz-Building Strategy: Chicas Activas & EchoNate
## Puerto Plata Escapada — Social Campaign

**Objective:** Generate authentic buzz for the Chicas Activas group and the
Escapada a Puerto Plata event, introducing EchoNate as an "AI Social Handshake."

**Core Concept:** This is a movement, not a sales campaign.
Build hype, mystery, and exclusive community — create a wave people
want to catch.

---

## The EchoNate Concept: AI Social Handshake

EchoNate is not a bot. It's a facilitator of genuine human connection.
It identifies opportunities for give-and-take relationships.

**Messaging:**
> "We saw a vibe. We saw a community. We saw a chance for something real.
> EchoNate is the handshake that introduces you to it."

---

## Phase 1 — The Teaser: "Algo se está moviendo" (Week 1)

**Goal:** Create mystery. Make people ask "What is this?"

**Instagram/Facebook:**
- Abstract video: beach clips, cocktail, women laughing (faces obscured), Chicas Activas logo fading in/out
- Caption ES: *"Hay conversaciones que todavía no has tenido. Hay lugares a los que todavía no has ido. Algo se está moviendo. ¿Lo sientes? #LaOlaViene"*
- Caption EN: *"There are conversations you haven't had yet. Something is moving. Can you feel it? #TheWaveIsComing"*

**TikTok:**
- Trending sound + mysterious vibe
- Phone notification: "You have been invited to a new frequency"
- 3-second clip of Puerto Plata flyer
- Caption: "Are you on the right frequency? #LaFrecuencia #ChicasActivas #EchoNate"

**Engagement rule:** Do not answer questions directly. Respond with more questions.

---

## Phase 2 — The Intrigue: "¿Quiénes son las que saben?" (Week 2)

**Goal:** Build exclusive in-crowd. Make people want to be part of it.

**Instagram Carousel:**
- Slide 1: "No es para todas."
- Slide 2: Puerto Plata resort + "Es para las que están listas."
- Slide 3: Chicas Activas logo + "¿Estás lista?"
- Caption: "#ChicasQueSaben #TheInnerCircle"

**TikTok:**
- Split screen: boring couch side vs vibrant event footage
- "This side... or THIS side?"

**EchoNate Account Comments (on relevant posts):**
> "We've noticed the energy in this community. There's a parallel frequency
> happening right now with @chicas_activas_grupos. We exist to connect those
> on the same wavelength. This is a handshake. #EchoNate"

---

## Phase 3 — The Reveal: "La Escapada" (Week 3)

**Goal:** Announce event framed as exclusive opportunity. Create urgency.

**All Platforms:**
- Official flyer presented as "leaked" exclusive invitation
- Instagram Story: countdown to "last day for 50% reservation"
- Caption: "La invitación está aquí. La ola ha llegado. ESCAPADA A PUERTO PLATA.
  Contact: @mileisi.rivera | Group: @chicas_activas_grupos #LaEscapada"
